About the ministry

A voice of revival.

✦ Multi-award-winning

Butho Vuthela is a multi-award-winning gospel artist, motivational speaker, preacher, producer and songwriter. African rhythms are woven through a contemporary Christian sound, carrying one assignment — to lift up the name of Jesus.

This is a living ministry catalogue: music for worship, reflection, testimony and the shared journey of faith.
